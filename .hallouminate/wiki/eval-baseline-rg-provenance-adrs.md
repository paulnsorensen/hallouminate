# ADRs — eval-baseline-rg-provenance

### ADR-001: Re-baseline now despite the divergence's cause remaining unexplained  [status: accepted]
- **Context:** Three `/pasteurize` rounds falsified all five ranked hypotheses (path-leaked tie-breaks, dropped signal, model-cache drift, rg version/schema skew, per-file cap) for why the committed baseline (MRR 0.8250) doesn't reproduce (measured 0.7660, 30/73 queries changed top-chunk). Full trail: `.cheese/pasteurize/eval-request-envelope-regression.md`. The weekly "Ground retrieval evaluation" CI job is red in the meantime.
- **Decision:** Once `ripgrep_version` provenance lands (spec: `eval-baseline-rg-provenance`), re-measure and re-commit `eval/baseline.json` on CI's own environment, accepting the new numbers as the baseline going forward rather than continuing to chase the original divergence.
- **Alternatives:** Keep the baseline as-is and the CI job red/skip-marked until the cause is found — rejected by the user; risks leaving ranking-regression detection blind indefinitely with no proven path to resolution, since all cheap hypotheses are exhausted and the next step (recovering the original measuring machine's exact environment) isn't achievable.
- **Consequences:** Buys an unblocked, green CI gate immediately. Costs the possibility that the 0.8250→0.7660 shift reflects a real (if narrow) ranking-quality regression that ships silently baked into the new baseline — mitigated only by the fact that recall@5 is unchanged throughout (only MRR/rank-within-tie moved), and by this ADR making the trade-off visible to future readers rather than hiding it.

### ADR-002: Record ripgrep version forensically, do not enforce or pin  [status: accepted]
- **Context:** `eval/baseline.json`'s provenance block only ever recorded `embedding: {model, quantized, cache_dir}` — nothing about `rg`, which is a PATH-resolved, unversioned peer ranked signal in fusion since #293 (`crates/hallouminate-domain/src/search/ripgrep.rs:111`). This gap is why the MRR divergence investigated in `.cheese/pasteurize/eval-request-envelope-regression.md` couldn't be fully diagnosed — hypothesis #4 (rg version/schema skew) was ruled out locally but stayed unfalsifiable against whatever machine produced the committed baseline, since nothing recorded its `rg` version.
- **Decision:** Add a single `ripgrep_version: String` field to `EvalArtifact`, populated from `rg --version`'s first output line, recorded but never compared in `compare_against_baseline` — matching the existing record-only pattern already used for `query_set_digest`.
- **Alternatives:** (B) record a fuller tool/environment fingerprint (rg + rustc + OS/arch) — more forensic reach than the diagnosed problem needs today, rejected as unproportionate. (C) record + enforce a pinned version, failing the eval on drift — rejected because no evidence ties an rg version bump to an actual ranking-behavior change; enforcing on an unproven correlation risks blocking CI on noise. (D) vendor an exact `rg` binary for the eval harness — heaviest, rejected as disproportionate to a forensic-recording goal.
- **Consequences:** A future divergence like this one becomes diagnosable (or at least the rg-version hypothesis becomes falsifiable) without another multi-round pasteurize chase. Does not, by itself, prevent divergence — only makes it explainable after the fact. Scope stays eval-harness-only; production `ripgrep.rs` is explicitly untouched (a separate, bigger decision if ever pursued).
